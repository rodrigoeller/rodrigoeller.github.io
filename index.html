Leo & Lully Almanacs

Editorial Design · Art Direction · Character-Based IP · Educational Products

Overview

An activity-almanac collection created around the Leo & Lully IP, introducing preschool-age children to the five senses through playful, family-friendly activities.

The goal was to translate an established children's entertainment property into a cohesive physical publishing product: playful and recognizable, yet clear and practical for parents and kids.

My role

  • Graphic Design
  • Art Direction
  • Editorial Design System
  • Character Application

Impact

  • Published by Editora 52
  • Five-day learning journey per volume
  • 15 connected activities per volume
  • Printable activity kit
  • Parent guidance & conversation prompts

Building the visual system

A shared editorial framework: a strong purple-and-pink navigation system, expressive display typography, recurring graphic markers, clear activity labels and a flexible page structure. Each volume gets its own theme and imagery while the collection stays one recognizable family.

Designing for use

Five days, three connected activities per day — plus a parent introduction, a visual "how to use this book" guide, printable sheets, material indicators, "Fica a Dica" notes for adults and "Perguntas Curiosas" conversation prompts.

Character applications

Leo, Lully and supporting characters act as guides, participants and visual anchors. Their expressions, poses and interactions reinforce each activity and keep a strong connection to the original IP.
